Photovoltaic façades: BIPV and BAPV

Photovoltaic panels can be mounted on top of an existing façade (BAPV) or can replace the construction material itself (BIPV). Informative overview, based on the FAERS/ZAKH manufacturer's documentation.

BAPV

BAPV — Building Applied Photovoltaics

Solar panels installed on top of an existing façade or roof. They do not replace the construction material but are attached to it using mounting systems.

  • Easy installation on new or existing buildings
  • Lower cost and faster implementation
  • Lightweight structure
  • Ideal for retrofit projects and buildings with finished façades

BIPV

BIPV — Building Integrated Photovoltaics

Solar panels that become part of the building's structure and replace traditional materials such as glass, stone or metal cladding.

  • Dual function: architectural façade and power generation
  • Highest aesthetic integration
  • Thermal and sound insulation
  • Modern design
