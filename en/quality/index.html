Quality & Compliance

Our approach emphasizes product traceability, documented quality checks, applicable safety requirements and reliable project documentation.

PrinciplesCertifications

We prioritize accurate product information, traceable documentation and transparent communication throughout our projects and customer relationships.

  • 01

    Traceability

    Product information and documents that can be traced back to source.

  • 02

    Documented checks

    Recorded quality checks rather than general statements.

  • 03

    Safety requirements

    Compliance with the safety requirements applicable to each product and project.

  • 04

    Project documentation

    Clear and complete documentation for every project.

  • IEC 61215

    Design qualification and type approval for crystalline silicon PV modules

  • IEC 61730

    Safety qualification of PV modules

  • IEC 61701

    Resistance to salt mist corrosion

  • IEC 62804

    Potential induced degradation (PID) resistance

  • IEC 62716

    Resistance to ammonia corrosion

  • ISO 9001

    Quality management system

  • ISO 14001

    Environmental management system

  • ISO 45001

    Occupational health and safety management system

  • CE

    European conformity marking
