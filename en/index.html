The manufacturer

ZAKH Renewable Energy & Engineering Manufacturing FZ-LLC

FAERS modules are manufactured by ZAKH at its production site in the RAKEZ free zone in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. According to the manufacturer, the photovoltaic panel line has a capacity of 600 MW, and a second line is dedicated to BIPV façade systems.

About our approach

Carbon & energy

Three instruments that are often confused

Voluntary credits, EU ETS allowances and renewable-energy certificates follow different rules. We explain them, with no promises of returns.

  • 01

    Voluntary carbon credits

    Voluntary market; not a permit to emit

  • 02

    Regulated EU ETS allowances

    Regulated instrument; not interchangeable with voluntary credits

  • 03

    Renewable-energy certificates

    Evidence origin, not an emission reduction in themselves
